Bio

Yair Walton builds programs and the operating systems underneath them: partnership networks, outcome measurement, data infrastructure, and the teams that hold all of it together. He is Senior Rabbi and Director of Program Operations and Strategy for K'vod Connect at Jewish Family Service of Cincinnati, an initiative working to end social isolation among older adults, and has led the program since its 2020 launch, working with a multidisciplinary team across community programming, spiritual care, volunteer engagement, and evaluation.

The program

K'vod Connect is an initiative of Jewish Family Service of Cincinnati. It runs on two tracks: community based programs across the city, including gatherings, transportation support, and one on one visits from trained volunteers, and programming embedded inside partner retirement communities. Spiritual care runs through both.

More than 1,300 older adults have been reached since the program launched in 2020. In 2025 it received the Outstanding Project in the Field of Aging award from the Tristate Association for Professionals in Aging.
